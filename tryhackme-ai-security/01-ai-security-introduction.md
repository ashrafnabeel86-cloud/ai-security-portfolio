## What I Learned

### 1. AI Security
AI security focuses on protecting AI systems, machine learning models, data, prompts, and outputs from misuse, manipulation, or attacks.

### 2. Why AI Security Matters
AI systems can be attacked in different ways. Attackers may try to manipulate prompts, poison training data, extract sensitive information, or misuse AI tools for harmful purposes.

### 3. Defensive AI
AI can also help defenders by supporting log analysis, phishing detection, malware analysis, threat detection, and security automation.
